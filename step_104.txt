### 9. Collective Intelligence / User Scam Reporting (`brain.md` §32)
* **What it is:** A *"Report this recipient"* button on the UI. When clicked, it logs a scam report, increases the dispute count for that UPI ID in the database, and immediately raises the reputation risk for any future payment to that ID.
